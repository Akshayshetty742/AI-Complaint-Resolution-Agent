COMPANY LIMITED WARRANTY POLICY

1. Overview:
This policy outlines warranty protection for electronic devices, appliances, and hardware products sold on our platform.

2. Warranty Period and Coverage:
- Standard Warranty: 12 months (1 year) from the date of initial purchase on all electronic devices, laptops, headphones, and appliances.
- Extended Warranty: If purchased, extends coverage by an additional 12 or 24 months.
- Coverage includes: Manufacturing defects, hardware malfunctions, internal component failures, power/battery failure not caused by liquid damage, and firmware glitches.

3. Exclusions (Not Covered):
- Physical impact damage, accidental drops, cracked screens, or water/liquid submersion.
- Unauthorized third-party repairs or modifications.
- Normal wear and tear (cosmetic scratches, battery degradation below expected thresholds after 12 months).

4. Warranty Claim Process:
- Customer provides Order ID, serial number, and a brief description of the issue.
- Technical Support performs remote diagnostic troubleshooting.
- If repair is necessary, customer is given a prepaid shipping label to the authorized service center.
- Turnaround time for warranty repair is 7 to 10 business days. If unrepairable, an equivalent replacement unit or store credit is provided.

5. Escalation Conditions:
- If a warranty repair exceeds 15 business days without status update, escalate to Warranty Operations Manager for expedited replacement unit dispatch.
- Repeated failures (same issue occurring 3+ times under warranty) escalate to Technical Quality Assurance for product recall assessment or full refund approval.
