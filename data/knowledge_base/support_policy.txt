COMPANY GENERAL CUSTOMER SUPPORT POLICY

1. Overview:
This policy outlines support operational hours, channels, response service-level agreements (SLAs), and issue resolution workflows.

2. Support Channels and Operating Hours:
- Live Chat & AI Agent: Available 24/7/365.
- Email Support: Response within 12 to 24 hours.
- Phone Support: Available Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 8:00 PM EST.

3. Service Level Agreements (SLAs) by Priority:
- High Priority (Damaged Goods, Urgent Delays, Payment/Account Access Issues): First response within 1 hour; resolution target 24 hours.
- Medium Priority (Returns, Warranty Inquiries, General Tracking): First response within 4 hours; resolution target 48 hours.
- Low Priority (General feedback, Product inquiries, Account settings): First response within 24 hours; resolution target 72 hours.

4. Escalation Conditions:
- If a customer contacts support 3 or more times regarding the same unresolved ticket, escalate immediately to Senior Customer Advocacy Team.
- Profanity or abusive interactions are flagged and transferred to Specialized Conflict Resolution Supervisors.
- Requests where company policy does not have clear coverage must be logged as "Policy Review Request" and escalated to Support Operations Lead for manual determination.
