COMPANY DELIVERY AND SHIPPING POLICY

1. Overview:
This policy outlines shipping methods, expected delivery windows, delays, tracking, and non-delivery protocols.

2. Delivery Timelines:
- Standard Shipping: 3 to 5 business days from shipment confirmation.
- Express Shipping: 1 to 2 business days from shipment confirmation.
- Rural/Remote Regions: Up to 7 to 10 business days.
- Real-time tracking numbers are provided via email and SMS within 24 hours of dispatch.

3. Delays and Non-Delivery:
- If an order is delayed past the estimated delivery date by more than 48 hours, customers receive automated shipping credits.
- An order marked "Delivered" that has not arrived must be reported within 48 hours for a lost-in-transit courier investigation.
- Lost-in-transit courier investigations take a maximum of 3 business days.

4. Escalation Conditions:
- If a delivery status remains unchanged / stuck in transit for more than 5 business days, escalate to Senior Logistics Dispatch.
- If courier marks package delivered but customer denies receipt after 72 hours of investigation, escalate to Customer Operations for emergency reshipment or refund authorization.
