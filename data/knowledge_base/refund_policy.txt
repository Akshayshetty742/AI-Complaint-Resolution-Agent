COMPANY REFUND POLICY

1. Overview:
This policy outlines the conditions under which customers are eligible for refunds for purchases made on our platform.

2. Eligibility Conditions:
- Orders cancelled prior to dispatch are eligible for an immediate full refund (100% of order value).
- Returns approved under the Return Policy are refunded once the item passes warehouse inspection.
- If a duplicate charge occurs, the excess payment will be refunded immediately upon verification.
- Digital services and downloadable products are strictly non-refundable once accessed.

3. Refund Timeframes and Methods:
- Credit/Debit Cards: 5 to 7 business days from approval date.
- UPI / Net Banking: 2 to 4 business days from approval date.
- Store Credit / Wallet: Instant credited within 24 hours.
- If a customer has not received their refund within 7 business days following cancellation or return approval, an automated trace ID will be generated.

4. Escalation Conditions:
- If a refund is delayed beyond 7 business days, the ticket must be escalated to Tier-2 Billing Support with the payment gateway transaction reference.
- Any unauthorized deduction claims must be escalated immediately to Fraud Prevention.
- Disputes exceeding $500 require Senior Finance Manager approval.
