COMPANY DAMAGED, DEFECTIVE, OR WRONG PRODUCT POLICY

1. Overview:
This policy applies to items received broken, physically damaged, defective out-of-the-box (DOA), or incorrect items delivered.

2. Reporting Window and Evidence Requirements:
- Damaged or broken items must be reported within 48 hours of confirmed delivery.
- Defective out-of-the-box items must be reported within 7 days of delivery.
- The customer must provide photographic or video evidence showing:
  a) The outer shipping box and courier shipping label.
  b) The damaged product displaying the damage or defect clearly.
  c) The serial number or model sticker where applicable.

3. Resolution Options:
- Immediate Replacement: Priority replacement dispatched within 24 to 48 hours at zero additional cost once photos are validated.
- Full Refund: If the item is out of stock, a 100% full refund is issued via the original payment method.
- Return of Damaged Item: The company may arrange pickup of the damaged unit; customer is not required to pay return shipping fees.

4. Escalation Conditions:
- If a replacement request is denied due to photo ambiguity, escalate to Tier-2 Damage Assessment Specialist for secondary review.
- If high-value items (over $300) arrive broken or damaged, escalate directly to the Risk and Fulfillment Manager.
