COMPANY RETURN POLICY

1. Overview:
Our return policy ensures customers can return eligible physical items within the specified return window.

2. Return Window and Eligibility:
- Standard physical products can be returned within 14 calendar days of delivery.
- Items must be unused, unwashed, with original tags intact, and in original packaging.
- Electronics must have all serial numbers legible and all bundled accessories included.
- Non-returnable items include: perishable goods, intimate apparel, customized/personalized items, and gift cards.

3. Return Procedure:
- Customers initiate returns via the customer portal or support agent.
- A prepaid shipping label or doorstep pickup will be arranged within 48 hours of request approval.
- Upon arrival at the warehouse, inspection takes 2 business days. If approved, replacement or refund is triggered.

4. Escalation Conditions:
- If a return pickup fails twice due to courier error, escalate to Logistics Management for courier reassignment.
- If an item rejected during inspection is disputed by the customer, escalate to Quality Assurance Supervision within 24 hours.
